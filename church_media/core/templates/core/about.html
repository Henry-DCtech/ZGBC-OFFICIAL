BIOGRAPHY

Apostle Sunday Henry Leboku, hailed from Mkpani, Aduma, Lebolkom Yakurr LGA, Cross River State, Nigeria,
born in the South-West into the family of Late Engr. Silvanus Ubangha Leboku and Late Mrs. Teresa Leboku on the 18th November, 1987 in a town called Modakeke, Osun State, Nigeria. He is the third child of his parents, he had five brothers.

He attended ST. Stephen’s ‘B’ Primary school 1993-1999. Modakeke High School for my Junior Secondary School 2000 – 2002, and he finished his SSCE at Gbongan/Odeomu Anglican School of Science between 2002 – 2005, later, sat for the National Examination Counsil (NECO) Exam at Ogunsua Grammar School, Modakeke in the year 2008.

SPIRITUAL JOURNEY

After SSCE around April 2005 he returned home. Between May-July of that year, a man appeared in my dream in the cloud wearing white robe, the hair of his head was white as wool and stood in the cloud, calling him to come up to him in the cloud and suddenly a force picked him up in other to meet with the man in the cloud. This incident happened three (3) more times and the fourth time the man came down to the earth, this time i was able to meet him and bow at his feet and he embraced me and smile then the vision went off.

Apostle Henry took another journey to Ogun state around July ending 2005 to find work to do, and he was able to secure a labor job at Covenant University. He worked with the bricklayers in those days. After one month of staying with one of his Dad‟s family member at Hall-Over Polytechnic, Omolabeke Estate, Sango-Ota, Ogun State, they threw him out because he refused to use the son account for the payment of the work he does at the site but God sent a young man name David who took him in and they were staying in the place prepared for the laborers. They were sleeping on a wood and they place a mat on it, he slept there for another three months. During this period he do attend Winners Chapel, Ota and one October during one of their mid-week services, Bishop David Oyedepo ministered and make altar call, he came out and surrender his life to Christ, and after Bishop prayed something enter into him, it‟s a burning faith he can‟t explain, it keeps drawing him and he bought one small KJV bible in those days he began to read and there he started unlocking things in the realm of the spirit but he was not satisfied.
He returned home after several signs and challenges at the site and a man told him and said; “I think it is time for you to go back home if you're seeing all this things. So, on the 12th of November, 2005 he finally returned home.

Frist Sunday on the year 2006, he joined a church name Gospel Faith Mission international, Ifelodun Assembly, Modakeke where he started his Christian journey fully, he went all the training, baptismal and worker training, but he was not satisfied as something is calling him within, so he approach the prayer coordinator that he wanted to join the prayer team and he said no problem but they examine him for one (1) years before he can join. So his one year came 2008 January and he was grafted into the team, then, God began to open him up to supernatural encounters and visions of heavens, angels of different kinds, angelic visitations and as well as encounter with different demonic spirit of all kinds. He began to grow stronger in the team and more so, he do go to mountains with the CAC people and there he learn how stay on the mountain without food for days, weeks etc. He prayed in those days seeking the power of God on daily basis, towards the end of 2008 he began to have encounter with God‟s general Pastor E.A. Adeboye.
He will see him in his dream, and he called him saying son follow me; then he began to show him how to cast out demons and evil spirit from people and heal them. He saw a vision one day, heaven opened and a white dove came out of the cloud and divided itself to several doves and move to all the earth in twos. Then, two doves came me in my vision as I was watching and they spoke with man‟s voice saying “Preach that the kingdom of heaven is at hand” Matthew 3:2. God gave me another vision during this time; he saw three general overseers of different ministries in Nigeria namely Pastor E.A Adeboye, Pastor E.O Abina, and Bishop David Oyedepo sitting in an office doing meeting and he saw that he was standing at the door to the office waiting for them. What they‟re discussion he don‟t know, but he stood at the entrance door waiting as Joshua did to Moses at the entrance of the mountain. This encounter kept happening for time he can't count till 2010 that he entered university.

UNIVERSITY MINISTRY

On the night of 28th / 29th January 2010, he saw the vision of the Lord and it goes thus; he was going on a street in the vision and suddenly he entered another street and he saw crowed of people too many to number, but in the midst of the people were men in white robe very many, he said to himself he must see what is happening there, upon entering into the midst of the people to really see what they‟re doing, he saw something like aluminum drums was place on fire, it depict how palm oil is being produced , they‟re boiling the oil inside this drums in different places, while he was watching and looking suddenly, a white and very bright light came from heaven and hit him on the forehead he closed his eyes because the strength of the light could affect his eyes, this light scanned him from his head to the toe and from toe back to his head, immediately after the scanning one of the men in white robe came and dipped a bow inside the drum and brought out anointing oil and pour it on my head and also said he should open my mouth and pour it in my mouth to drink as well.

He drank the oil and immediately began to speak in tongues so powerfully and suddenly my spiritual eyes was opened in that vision, he different Angels of God standing in the air gracing the coronation ceremony, he saw some angels with big Wings, while some didn't, an angel carried another baby angel in his arm and this baby angel was the singing but he could not understand the language nor learn the song, all he felt in that encounter was great power and authority, the angels were there for so long and after they‟re went back to heaven, he kept speaking in tongue till he woke up on his bed, this is how he received the baptism of the Holy spirit.

He returned to school on the 4th of February, 2010 for his lectures but on the 26th February, God sent a message to him that he has called him and he must do his work. He tried figuring out what to do but he couldn't. So to obey God, he then started a prayer meeting on campus which erupted into something he never imagined. People kept coming for the program from different source and he saw the power of God working through him in a dimension he can‟t explain, deliverance was just too easy for him, healings and other forms of ministrations as the Holy Spirit permitted him. He now joined himself with the Gospel Student Fellowship (GSF) being campus fellowship of his church where he service as a worker after GAA training and he was chosen as the prayer secretary 2011/2012 and finally served as Vice-President in the 2012/2013 before my graduation.

He was known as one of the campus leader whom many pastors have been raised from today, many strong sisters and brother in the Lord today to glory of Jesus Christ.

OFFICIAL CALL TO MINISTRY BEGINS

After graduation from university, he relocated to Akure for just one year before going for his National Youth Service, and during this period, he entered unprecedented season, and experienced things he never had any idea on and what will be the end of it. Suddenly, he found himself alone separated, no friend, no companion, no relative and suddenly nobody was there again to support him financially and to make the matter worse, he began to search for work in those days but nobody employed him for the reason he can't explain. He search for driving job, got none, One faithful morning he prepared another application letters as usual, no money for transport so he began to trekking distance without food, no money to even buy pure water N10.

So as he was going on the road, he began complaining to God and said, Lord why did you allow me to working on the street like this? And immediately a voice came out of the cloud and said to him “Who send you on the road? When he heard that voice he paused for a moment, then turned back to his house trekking again and dropped the job hunting stuff. In those he was only feeding on whatever God provides, but he created more time to study scriptures and be with God alone in the house for several months, my spiritual life grew strong than ever know and he had strong intimacy with the Holy spirit. Then after some times, the Holy Spirit told him to leave his room and go the mountain, and said to do what, the Spirit of God answered just go and he pick up his clothes and went the mountain along Idanre Road, Akure.

He went to the mountain and on getting there; he told me to go and meet the prophet that is ministering to people over there. He did not know the man and they have never met. So he went to meet with the man as led by the Holy Spirit and told him he was asked to come and meet with him, the man asked who, he said the Holy Spirit, then, he responded, let's pray and after the prayer the man of God told him point black that if he keep searching for job from now till years he can't find one even primary school teaching. Henry then asked why sir and he responded again because “Ministry is your Work” and he added just pack your things and go to Bible College.

HOW HE BECAME A MEMBER OF REDEEMED CHRISTIAN CHURCH OF GOD

He left the mountain sad but he don't have option, he called his parent to inform them of the move of God in his life, but as human they objected and refuse to give him money again form sometimes, the case start to get worst, so he decided to take a form in one international non-denominational bible school where his spiritual leader went to, but he tried all he could no money to pay. So he went to pray and ask God where to do the bible college after consulting with one of his spiritual mother in Akure, and in a vision the Lord used him restore a whole village then they asked him where he is from and he said he is a pastor in Redeemed Christina Church of God. He told his spiritual mother and she said that‟s it go there for your bible college. The day he was to purchase the form, his Late Dad sent him N5000.00 for monthly upkeep, so he used the money to get the form and this is how he enter into RCCG. But while he was asking question in the spirit because he don‟t want to stay there at all, but he was told to stay there for now. He started all over again from workers in training, baptismal class, School of Discipleship, Bible College (RCBC) all at the same time. After my Bible College graduation April 28th, 2015, I returned home and prepared for my NYSC.

Apostle Henry then stayed in a town called Ogbese along Owo Road, Ondo State with a man of God named Pastor Tayo Ojo, and in those days he was in his house fasting and praying to God for the purpose of God to be fully known and these happened for some months and on the 21st June, 2015 the Lord revealed to him in a vision, a scroll was giving onto him and inside the scroll was an inscription written in capital letter and when open the scroll he found what was written which says MINISTRY from time onward, Apostle Henry knew that he has been called into the ministry for real.

HIS JOURNEY TO SOKOTO FOR NYSC AND THE SPIRITUAL ENCOUNTERS

Apostle Henry went for his NYSC October 26th, 2015 and on getting to Sokoto, the Lord began to show him different vision of ministry and even despite the fact that the place I was transferred to, we were not allow to carry bible yet, we stayed with God. he was stoned in the chest on one faithful Sunday like that while he was preaching in the Lodge but we kept doing it to the glory of God. One day, the Lord gave me a vision and in the vision he was carrying a white envelope in my hand without knowing what was inside and bible on the other hand, He was putting on suit and going on a road. As much as he tried to move the journey was just too slow for him, so he prayed to God to help him and give me strength, suddenly a force carried him up and took him very fast into a long distance and dropped him in front of a church, he went straight into the church and upon entering I saw four people in the church which is Rev‟d Olusola Areogun and other three Elders. He gave him the letter in my hand and he opened it. After reading it the man of God said to him and others that it's a letter to ministry and the vision went off after he told the elders to give him what to do.

After my NYSC, he returned back home and the same thing that happened 2014 started again. He started looking for a job but couldn't get one again. He lost an interview at the last stage to become a lecturer at Odudua University November 2016 all because he has not started his Masters, but they were employing others with BSc. And they rejected him. After few days he saw a terrible vision and when he made enquiry again he was told to obey God and go into ministry. This is how he applied officially November 2016 and he started as an assistant parish pastor which he continued for 4 year and 10months and during this year he went to School of Mission (RECSOM) and House Fellowship Leadership Training, he was ordained as Deacon August 2019 and he was posted to pastor the first church RCCG Kings Honor on the 10th October, 2021. He was ordained as an Assistant Pastor August 2024. But all this years, he has been receiving Angelic visitations and several messages about his ministry, especially the year 2020.

ZION GLOBAL BIBLE CHURCH WAS REVEALED TO HIM

The year 2020, the show him that he's going to have his own church which he wept in the dream because he did not want it, due a lot of responsibility and after some years the name of the ministry was reveal to him in a vision. After conducting deliverance for someone in his dream and it was successfully, suddenly his eyes opened and he saw what the Lord wrote in the cloud, very bold and clear ZION GLOBAL BIBLE CHURCH, these was the second time the name will be revealed because the first time he forgot it before waking up.
He continue to struggle with it for years and things started going worse for him, everything start crashing, he was faced with serious hated, jealousy, and envy from different level of ministers till the day he resigned. He faced several spiritual attacks but he sees the Lord in it all. So November 2024, the Lord gave him last warning to leave RCCG and he had received several warning in his vision which he documented for privacy sake. He wrote his resignation letter January 2025, so when the occasion presented itself and permitted him to leave, he then resigned officially on the 8th of July 2025 to face the new life and the ministry God gave him to fulfill.

By divine instruction he was instructed to move to Osogbo and on getting there he started seeing the same man of God, Rev'd Areogun again, he then told like three(3) spiritual leaders he trusted and they all pointed back to him that he must see the man of God and tell him what God shown to him 2016 in Sokoto, and receive the final instruction and mentorship from him before setting out finally for ministry.

This is the summary of his spiritual journey before entering into his personal ministry fully.
